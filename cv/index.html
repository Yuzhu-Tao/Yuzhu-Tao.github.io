Projects

  • Spherical-Projection Panoramic Image Stitching System for Data Centers

    Developed a fast spherical-projection panoramic stitching system for data-center scenarios.

    • Problem: a single camera has limited field of view in data-center scenes, requiring panoramic reconstruction from multiple views.
    • Solution: proposed a fast spherical-projection panoramic stitching algorithm, corrected lens distortion, estimated focal length via cylindrical projection, and built a spherical projection model to fuse 30 images into an equirectangular projection panorama supporting 3D viewing.
    • Result: reconstructs a full panorama in one second and was deployed in China Tower’s production business.
    • Authored one patent related to the project, owned by China Tower.

  • Research Statement

    My research lies at the intersection of geometric computer vision and generative image modeling. Through my stitching work (TCSVT) and panoramic reconstruction (ACM MM), I identified large parallax as a fundamental limitation of alignment-based methods, and I am interested in exploring image editing models for local parallax region regeneration. During my internship, I observed shared texture fidelity challenges across tasks: VAE decoders losing high-frequency detail in ID-photo generation, tiling artifacts in repetitive textures such as grass, and the inability of super-resolution models to recover semantic information. These observations motivate my PhD research interest: understanding and solving texture-faithful generative editing under geometric constraints.
